Frequently asked

What is fips-exit?
A paid gateway that lets devices on the fips mesh reach the regular Internet. You buy a prepaid data package and browse through the exit; your nostr identity is your account.
How do I sign in?
With your nostr identity — no email or password. If you're connecting over fips, you can usually just confirm your npub (it's verified against your fips address, no signature needed). Otherwise use a browser extension (NIP-07), or paste a signed event from any nostr signer.
How is my data measured?
By the bytes that pass through the exit to the Internet, counted against your package. Different services count at different rates — see “The two ways to connect” above. Traffic that stays inside the fips mesh is never metered.
What can I pay with?
Bitcoin. Lightning payments unlock access instantly. On-chain Bitcoin unlocks once the payment confirms (about one block). The exact methods offered appear at checkout.
Can I use the same package on my other devices?
Yes. On your dashboard, add another device or person by their npub under “Shared access” — they’ll draw on the same balance. You can disable any of them at any time.
Why did a sign-in page appear while I was browsing?
That’s the exit telling you it can’t let this traffic through yet — either you’re not signed in, or your package has run out or expired. Sign in and (if needed) top up, and you’ll be back online.
Do I need to change any browser settings?
Use our ready-made proxy auto-config (PAC): it keeps .fips sites direct and sends the Internet through the exit, with remote DNS on (which is what lets .onion work and avoids DNS leaks). {{if .PacURL}}

  • Firefox: Settings → Network Settings → Automatic proxy configuration URL → paste the PAC URL above.
  • Chrome / Chromium: launch with --proxy-pac-url=<the PAC URL>.
Both have proxy/remote DNS on by default. {{else}} Ask your operator for the PAC URL, then set it as your browser's automatic proxy configuration URL. {{end}}
Can I reach .onion sites?
Yes — the Connectivity service reaches .onion addresses on the same connection, no extra setup. For full anonymity for all your traffic, use the Privacy service.
Do you log what I visit?
No. The exit does not log destinations — only per-account byte totals needed for billing. Your browsing history isn’t recorded.
What happens when my data runs out or my pass expires?
Access stops and you’ll see the sign-in/top-up page again. Buy another package to continue — Lightning gets you back online immediately.
I paid but I’m still not online.
Lightning is instant; on-chain Bitcoin needs a confirmation first. Check “Pending payments” on your dashboard — it shows whether a payment is still confirming.
