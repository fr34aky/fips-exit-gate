How it works

  • Activate the tunnel on the device and all its Internet traffic goes through the exit — IPv4, IPv6, UDP, every app. fips itself (.fips sites, this portal) stays direct.
  • The tunnel only works while this identity has an active package; otherwise the handshake is simply dropped (the app shows no recent handshake). Top up here, over fips, and it connects on its next retry.
  • Traffic is metered per fips identity like the proxy services, including the tunnel's own overhead (a few percent). One configuration per device.
